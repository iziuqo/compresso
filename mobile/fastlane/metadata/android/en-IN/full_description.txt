Photos too big to send? Compresso makes them smaller in one tap, right on your phone.

Pick one photo or a hundred. Say what they're for. Compresso picks the right size and keeps them looking great.

MADE FOR WHAT YOU'RE DOING
• Message: about 400 KB each, perfect for chat apps and texts
• Email: all your photos together under 5 MB
• Upload form: under the limit a website asks for, like 1 MB or 500 KB
• Free up space: much smaller copies that still look like the original
• Web: light, fast images for your site or shop

SIMPLE ENOUGH FOR EVERYONE
• Big, clear buttons and plain words
• Works with your phone's larger text size and screen reader
• No sliders to guess at. Pick a purpose and you're done

YOUR PHOTOS STAY YOURS
• Everything happens on your phone. Nothing is uploaded, ever
• Your originals are never changed or deleted
• Location can be removed before you share
• No account, no ads, no tracking

DONE RIGHT
• Never makes a photo bigger
• Keeps the date the photo was taken, so your library stays in order
• Works with HEIC, JPEG, PNG and WebP
• Compare before and after with a simple slider
• Available in English, Español, Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Português and 简体中文

Compresso is built on compresso.js, the open-source image library.
